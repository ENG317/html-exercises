Website Mission

The purpose of this website is to expose young adult readers and writers to contemporary and classic works of fiction writing. Through my reviews and book descriptions, I hope to inspire young adult readers to give reading the story a shot themselves and join in on the timeless discussions surrounding each piece!

Book and Author of the Month

The book of the month is A Farewell to Arms by Ernest Hemingway.

Ernest Hemingway (July 21, 1899 - July 2, 1961), was a Nobel and Pulitzer prize winning author known for a variety of fiction novels, short stories, and non-fiction titles. Notably, he served in the Red Cross and as an ambulance driver during World War I, and spent much of his professional life as a journalist/reporter. His familiarity and close proximity to war and foreign conflict is often highlighted throughout his work.
