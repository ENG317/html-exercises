About The Team

Kicking ass and taking names since 1994! We're the best damn time the 919 has to offer. As a club team at NC State University we compete at the DIAA level through National Collegiate Rugby and play colleges from across the country.

Join The Team

We are always looking for new players! If you are interested in joining the team, please fill out the form below and we will get back to you as soon as possible. We welcome players of all skill levels and experience. Whether you are a seasoned player or new to the sport, we would love to have you on our team. Come be a part of the Pack!

Show Your Support!

Want to show your support for the team? There are many ways to get involved! You can attend games or events, purchase concessions and team merchandise, or make a donation to help support our players and programs. Every little bit helps!
