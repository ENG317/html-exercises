Basics that Everyone Should Have in their Wardrobe

In an age where fast fashion is a consistent thorn in true style's side, it's important to create a wardrobe that is both sustainable and timeless.
  • A basic white tee
  • A pair of dark wash jeans
  • A versatile belt
  • A classic blazer
  • A pair of walking shoes

    • What's Already in your Closet?

    Before you go out and buy new clothes, take a look at what you already have in your closet. You may be surprised at how many items you can create outfits with.

    How to Thrift Like a Pro

    There are several tricks to thrifting that can help you find the best pieces.

    1. Learn to identify quality fabrics and construction.
    2. Know your body type and what styles flatter you.
    3. Don't be afraid to mix and match different pieces.
