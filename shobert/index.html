About Me

Hello, I'm Kailyn! I'm originally from a small town outside of Charlotte, North Carolina but moved to Raleigh, NC for to attend NC State University. I love to read and share my love of books with others. My aspirations to work in publishing stem from my love of reading and interest in the business side of the industry. Thus driving me to not only study English but also Business Administration.

What You'll Find Here

On this website, you'll find a collection of my favorite books, along with reviews and recommendations. I hope to create a community of book lovers who can share their thoughts and opinions on various literary works. You'll also find information about my professional background, including my resume and work samples, as I pursue a career in publishing.
