Strawberry Shortcake Cheesecake

  • Menu
  • Home Page

This recipe will leave you with 2 servings of cake.

Ingredents

Crust

  • Vanilla Waffers
  • Unsalted Butter
  • Sugar

Vanilla Cake

  • Moist French Vanilla cake Mix

Cheesecake

  • 8 ounces of cream cheese
  • 3 room temperature eggs
  • Unsalted Butter
  • Sugar
  • Non dairy heavy whipping cream

Materials

  • 2 9x9in round baking pans
  • 9in steel sring form pan
  • Mixing Bowl with mixing spoon or electric mixer
  • Flat cookie sheet or some pan large enough to hold spring form pan

Instructions

Crust

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Prep your cheesecake dish by spreading the pan with softened butter then shifting baking flour around the inside. This helps removal of your cake. For this portion you will be only using the detatable bottom part of the pan. Grind half a box of vanilla wafers until consistancy is sand like. Mix with sugar and melted butter, press onto the bottom of a cheesecake dish until bottom is fully and evenly covered. Place the pan on a baking rack and bake in the oven for 10 minuetes. When done let cool in the fridge.

Vanilla Cake

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Prep your both of your 9x9in baking pans by spreading the pans with softened butter then shifting baking flour around the inside. This helps removal of your cake.Follow the instructions on the cake mix box adding in one tablespoon of vanilla. Bake for the recommended time. When complete allow cake to cool and cut one of the cakes in half. The other can be set aside for the same cake or another dessert of your choosing.

Cheesecake

In a mixing bowl, mix together your softened cream cheese then mix in your heavy cream and vanilla. Reduce your mixing speed and add in one egg at a time until well mixed. Add in your granulated sugar and mix until smooth.

Assembly

Preheat your oven to 325 degrees. Take out your cooled crust that is still atached to the bottom of the spring form pan. If not done already, snap the sides of the pan into place. Add in about 3 scoops of your cheesecake batter to the pan. Add the cut layer of vanilla cake on top of your cheesecake. Place your strawberries ontop of your vanilla cake in any array you like. Add the equivalent of 3 scoops of cheesecake batter on top of the cake and strawberries. Repeat this process for your last cake half so your top layer is cheesecake. Add stawrberries on top if you would like.

Baking

Place a flat sheet under your pan incase there is any over flow. Bake at 325 degrees for 40 minutes then turn the oven off and leave in the oven with the door open for 20 minutes. Afterwords let cool in the fridge

This is a delicious dessert that combines the flavors of strawberry shortcake and cheesecake. It's perfect for any occasion!
