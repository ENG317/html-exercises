Save a Life Without a 15-Year Commitment

We connect college students with local animal shelters to provide temporary homes for pets in need. Our goal is to help reduce the number of animals in shelters while giving students the opportunity to experience the joy of pet ownership.

Common Myths

  • Myth: Living in an off-campus apartment disqualifies you.
    Fact: Most shelters welcome student renters with landlord approval!
  • Myth: Fostering is too expensive.
    Fact: Shelters provide food, crates, and medical care for free.
  • Myth: Fostering requires a long-term commitment that clashes with school breaks.
    Fact: Fostering is highly flexible, and shelters actively look for short-term volunteers.

How it Works

  1. Get roommate and landlord consent.
  2. Fill out a shelter application.
  3. Receive supplies and your temporary pet.
  4. Foster and save a life!
