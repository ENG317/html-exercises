Sour Candy

Our gumdrops are crafted with care, delivering that perfect balance of soft, pillowy chew and a delightful crunch from their sugar-coated exterior. Each bite bursts with vibrant fruit flavor — from classic cherry and lemon to tangy lime and sweet orange. Made with high-quality gelatin and natural fruit extracts, they offer a nostalgic taste that brings back childhood memories. Whether enjoyed on their own or used as a baking decoration, our gumdrops are a colorful addition to any occasion.

Rainbow Candies

Get ready for a chewy, colorful adventure! Our sweets are the rainbow of the candy world — soft, squishy, and packed with delicious fruit flavor in every bite. Coated in a sweet, slightly crunchy sugar shell, they’re the perfect sweet treat for snacking, baking, or just plain fun. Cherry, lemon, lime, orange — pick your favorite or grab them all!

Assorted Sweets

Gumdrops are soft, chewy candies coated in a sweet, tangy sugar shell. Made with gelatin and fruit-flavored syrups, they come in a rainbow of colors and flavors. Their classic dome shape and bouncy texture make them a timeless treat for all ages.
