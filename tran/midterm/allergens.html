Common Allergies

There are a number of sweets that are peanut free for those that don't want to have an allergic reaction

Our candies may contain or come into contact with the following major allergens:

  • Milk – Found in chocolate, milk chocolate, white chocolate, caramel, fudge, and creamy fillings.
  • Eggs – Present in some gummies, marshmallows, and glazed candies.
  • Soy – Used in lecithin (emulsifier) in chocolate and some candy coatings.
  • Wheat/Gluten – May be present in wafers, cookie pieces, malted candies, or certain chewy centers.
  • Peanuts – Used in peanut brittle, peanut clusters, and some nougat or truffle varieties.
  • Tree Nuts – Such as almonds, cashews, hazelnuts, pecans, and walnuts in pralines, truffles, and certain chocolates.
  • Sesame – Occasionally found in specialty or artisanal candies.
  • Mustard – Rare, but may appear in certain international or specialty confections.
  • Sulfites – Used as preservatives in some dried fruit-based candies and candied ginger.
  • Celery – May be present in certain savory candy blends or international products.
