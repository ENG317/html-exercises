7 Key Concepts for Your Financial Future

Investing 101

Investing involves putting away your money into assets that can grow your wealth overtime such as stocks, bonds, or funds. While investing can come with risk, understanding influencing factors such as diversification, time periods, and compound growth can help you make more informed decisions.

The Stock Market

The stock market is a marketpalce where investors can buy and sell shares of publicly traded companies. Stock prices can change frequently based on company performance, investor expectations, economic events, and levels of publicity.

Credit and Debt

Credit allows you to borrow money or make purchases with the promise to pay at a late date, while debt is theh amount you owe to a lender. Interest rates, credit scores, and payment requirements can help you use credit responsibly and avoid unncessary costs.

Taxes

Taxes are payments collected by governments to fund public services and programs. Your income, purchases, property, and other financial activities may be subject to different types of taxes, making it important to become aware about how taxes can affect your finances.

Retirement

Retirement planning involves saving and investing money that you can use when you are no longer working and choose to retire. Starting early can give your savings more time to grow through compound interet and aid in helping you prepare for future expenses.

Personal Finance

Personal finance is the process of managing your own income, spending, saving, and investing. Creating a budget and setting financial goals can help you understand where your money is going and make progress toward the things that matter to you.

Finance in the News

Financial news covers events that in turn can influence businesses, markets, and consumers. Events that cause changes in interest rates, inflation, employment, and economic growth can affect everything from investment prices to the cost of daily goods.
