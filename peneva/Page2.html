How Does Suki Help Users?

Suki provides a visually soothing companion that helps users destress by offering gentle color shifts, soft glow behaviors, and a calm atmosphere. She encourages users to slow down and engage with technology in a more intentional way, reducing overstimulation and digital fatigue. The glow, color palette, and movement patterns create a sense of visual grounding that helps users feel centered; Suki acts as a quiet, non-demanding being that provides comfort without requiring constant interaction.

Creator Info!

Suki was created by me, Deni, a 20 year old English major at NC State with a growing unhappiness of the state of technology. I want to see more creative, low-stakes technology that also considers the overstimulation current technology causes. I was deeply inspired by Creature's STARBOY, and I owe this project to their innovative approach. If you have any questions or feedback, feel free to reach out on GitHub!
