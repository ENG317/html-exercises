How Was Suki Created?

Suki is built intentionally, with foundational web technologies she runs smoothly without heavy frameworks or data tracking:

  1. HTML: Structured with semantic elements and ARIA landmarks to keep the page accessible to screen readers and keyboard navigation.
  2. CSS: Styled with transitions, custom properties, and ambient animations to create Suki's breathing glow states without harsh visual jumps.

How Does Suki Help Users?

Modern interfaces are often engineered to capture attention through urgency. Suki is designed to do the opposite. By offering gentle colour shifts, slow glow rhythms, and a quiet atmosphere, she encourages you to slow down and engage with your screen more intentionally.

Her movement and light patterns act as a visual anchor to reduce digital fatigue by giving you a calm, non-demanding presence on your screen.

Creator Info

Suki was created by me, Deni—a 20-year-old English major at NC State with a growing frustration toward the noisy state of modern technology. I want to see more creative, low-stakes software that respects human attention and actively works against overstimulation.

This project owes its heart to Creature Co's STARBOY and their thoughtful approach to personal tech. If you have questions, ideas, or feedback, feel free to reach out on GitHub!

What's Next for Suki?

As a solo student project, there are plenty of features still on my wish list—such as saving Suki's mood locally between visits, adding optional ambient soundscapes, and refining her sparkle animations.

The source code for Suki is open and available on GitHub. If you want to build upon her or suggest improvements, contributions are always welcome—as long as they stay true to her core purpose of being a calm, unintrusive companion.
