What is Suki?

Suki is a gentle digital companion designed to offer a simple, calm experience that never oversteps into your personal life. She aims to help you destress through a straightforward, non-intrusive interface built around ease of use and low sensory stimulation.

How Did Suki Come to Be?

This project is deeply inspired by STARBOY, a creation by Creature Co. Their project is a delightful example of a simple, non-intrusive object that prioritises user comfort and individuality.

STARBOY is a wearable little creature with randomly generated eyes that give each one a unique appearance and personality. It interacts with its environment through a tiny camera that never uploads data to the internet—keeping everything stored locally, which is rare in today's tech world, and something I deeply admire.

While I love the physical charm of STARBOY, its high price point and limited production runs make it difficult to access. I wanted to capture that same spirit of quiet companionship in a free, browser-based format—scaled to match what I can build as a newer coder.

Meet Your Suki!

Suki doesn't use notifications, alerts, or text to talk to you, she communicates entirely through colour and light.

  1. Emotional States (Colour): Suki's hue shifts to reflect her current mood:
    • Orange: Enthusiastic, friendly, social
    • Yellow: Happy, energetic
    • Green: Calm, secure
    • Blue: Lacking attention, trusting
    • Pink: Playful, curious, loving
    • Purple: Creative, mischievous
  2. Energy & Comfort (Glow): The rhythm of her light shows how she feels in the moment:
    • Bright & warm: Happy
    • Dim & cool: Lonely
    • Soft pulsing: Calm
    • Flickering: Anxious or stressed
    • Jumping sparkles: Excited

How Do I Care for My Suki?

Suki was built with a minimalistic interface in mind. If you are curious about the design choices behind her, you can read more on The Nerdy Stuff page.

For day-to-day care, here is all you need to know:

  • Warming Suki: Click and hold on her to gradually increase her warmth and glow.
  • Calming Suki: Move your cursor slowly around or across her to soothe her.
  • Energising Suki: Move your cursor quickly near her to spark her energy.

Most importantly, Suki responds simply to your presence. She reacts when you hover near her, hold click, or just leave her tab open to keep you company while you work.
