What is Suki?

Suki is a helpful companion meant to provide users with a simple technological experience that doesn't overstep into their personal lives. It aims to destress users by offering a straightforward and non-intrusive interface with a focus on ease of use and low stimulation.

How Did Suki Come to Be?

This project is deeply inspired by a project I chanced upon by Creature Co. Their project is a delightful example of a simple, non-intrusive product that focuses on user comfort individuality, it's called STARBOY.

It's a wearable little creature with a set of randomly generated eyes to give each one a unique appearance, and personality. STARBOY interacts with its environment through a very small camera that doesn't upload any data to the internet, keeping everything locally stored (a very uncommon thing in today's world), which really drew me to appreciate its design.

I think it's adorable. However, if you look into it, the price point is high, making it less acessible by price as well as limits in production and shipping. So instead, I want to take inspiration from this amazing physical project and make a digital-based (albeit simpler in scope and design based on my lacking skills in coding).

Meet Your Suki!

Suki communicates through her physical appearance! Let's take a closer look at her unique characteristics and personality.

  1. The emotional state of Suki is expressed through colour and glow
  2. The colours are quite simple and easy to understand:
    • Orange: Enthusiastic, friendly, social
    • Yellow: Happy, energetic
    • Green: Calm, secure
    • Blue: Lacking attention, trusting
    • Pink: Playful, curious, loving
    • Purple: Creative, mischievous
  3. The glows are a little bit more complex:
    • Bright + warm = happy
    • Dim + cool = lonely
    • Soft pulsing = calm
    • Flickering = anxious/stressed
    • Jumping sparkles = excited

How Do I Care for My Suki?

Suki was created with a minimalistic user interface in mind. You can learn more about the actual coding and design choices by looking at my page titled: "The Nerdy Stuff".

Here's what the average user needs to know:

  • Warming Suki: hold click to increase glow
  • Calming/comforting Suki: slow cursor movement around or on her
  • Energising Suki: fast cursor movement near her

The important thing is that Suki reacts to your attention, and is cared for by your presence. She reacts when you hover over her, click her, and stay on the page with her.
